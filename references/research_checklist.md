# Research checklist — run on every scheduled row

Verify with web search before writing a cell. Uncertain price/time → Notes: "approx — re-check before trip".

| # | Check | Typical failure seen before |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Weekday closure of every attraction **and** restaurant vs the real weekday of that day | Gyeongbokgung on Tuesday; Seokyo Nanmyunbang closed Tuesday; tofu restaurants closed Wednesday |
| 2 | Opening hours, last entry, last ferry/bus vs scheduled end time | Nami last ferry |
| 3 | Fixed departures (city tour bus first run, KTX/ITX times, show/ceremony slots) | Gapyeong City Tour Bus first departure 9:00; guard-changing 10:00/14:00 |
| 4 | Transit time between consecutive rows, sized for the group (8 people = 2–3 taxis; crowded buses on weekends) | Bus 181 on Sunday |
| 5 | International departure: at airport ≥ 3h before; ≥ 2h buffer after last activity | Day 6 buffer shrank to ~1h10 after adding shopping |
| 6 | Hotel location vs assumed area (re-anchor gather points and legs) | Seoul hotel in Hoegi, not Myeongdong |
| 7 | Restaurants: real name + address + distance; backups also real; not "near station" when it is across town | Jeongjitgan was near Busan Station, not Nampo |
| 8 | Seasonal facts (foliage peak, sunset time, seasonal food) | Dalmaji-gil foliage peaks early Nov |
| 9 | Advance-booking items → Checklists "Before" + Budget tier `advance` with deadline | Blueline Park Sky Capsule sells out ~4 weeks ahead |

After any day swap or row move, re-run checks 1–5 on **both** affected days — a swap that fixes one conflict often creates another.
